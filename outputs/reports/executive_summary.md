# Executive Summary

The Predictive Forecasting of Care Load and Placement Demand project provides a structured analytics tool for estimating short-term care-load trends, discharge demand, and capacity pressure. The system converts daily operational data into forecasts, confidence intervals, model comparison reports, and surge-risk indicators.

For HHS-style stakeholders, the dashboard offers a clear view of current trends, expected near-term demand, and potential capacity stress. Users can upload a CSV dataset, select a forecast model, choose a 7-day, 14-day, or 30-day horizon, and review forecast outputs through interactive charts and KPI cards.

The solution is designed to support planning conversations around placement capacity, discharge coordination, and operational readiness. It is not a policy decision system; it is an analytical forecasting aid that helps teams identify emerging pressure earlier and compare model performance transparently.
